Proof of Control

Researcher: Karan Kedarnath Doiphode

Finding: Domain / Subdomain Takeover

Status: Proof of Concept Confirmed

Purpose: Authorized Security Testing and Responsible Disclosure

Security Impact

The affected hostname references a resource that is no longer properly controlled by the organization.

An unauthorized party may potentially claim the associated external resource and serve arbitrary content through the organization's trusted hostname.

Responsible Disclosure Notice

This page exists exclusively to demonstrate control of the affected hostname as part of security testing.

No credentials, personal information, financial information, cookies, authentication tokens, or other sensitive information are collected or stored.

No malicious scripts, redirects, phishing forms, malware, tracking mechanisms, or deceptive content are deployed.

Recommended Remediation

The affected organization should review the DNS configuration for this hostname and remove any obsolete or dangling records.

Any external hosting service referenced by the DNS record should remain under the organization's control or the DNS record should be removed.
